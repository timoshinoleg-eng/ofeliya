# OFELIYA presentation overhaul design

Authorized 2026-10-07 by the project owner: autonomous execution and delegated implementation/review until a concrete final result. Existing Kimi access may be used; secrets remain on the authorized device. Deliver a reviewable branch/PR; production deployment and merge remain outside this task.

## Intended result

Premium microscopic biopunk presentation: tactile magenta/green virion, cold ivory/cyan immune silhouettes, warm burgundy/amber organism, clear combat contacts, readable reward and threat language, audible coherent audio. Preserve the existing game and its mature presentation architecture.

## Contracts

- Phaser 3.90 + TypeScript + Vite; no additional renderer, shader dependency, GSAP, Matter or particle library.
- Preserve damage, HP, speed, authoritative collision world geometry, AI timers, spawn/score/RNG/campaign contracts, MAX/Telegram behavior and Joystick.ts.
- Camera postFX stays disabled. Effects are local, bounded and Canvas-safe. Reduced quality removes decoration, never actors or gameplay telegraphs.
- Keep controls, modal safe areas, pause/mute targets and first-run progression intact.
- Assets are project-generated. Record provenance and fallback behavior. Boot art must be bounded, cached, and packaged locally; no external runtime image requests.
- Diagnostics remain DEV/QA-only. No identity or secret data in evidence.
- Browser automation cannot prove real MAX Android/iOS performance or audio unlock. Preserve that external release gate.

## Components

1. VO-01 records actual canvas/CSS/GL/scale/text state at DPR1/2/3. Phaser RESIZE logical coordinates are not replaced with a speculative resolution knob.
2. Combat retains VfxBudget, ImpactDirector and four emitters. Baked colored particle frames provide Canvas/WebGL palette parity; directed contact sparks and membrane fragments explain hits/deaths. Decorative shapes have a hard cap and deterministic teardown.
3. Organ atmosphere keeps current object counts and adds coherent deep/middle/near flow bands. Warm baked vessel detail remains behind gameplay. Heart gets two separated visual heartbeat peaks and a retained pulse overlay.
4. Audio keeps seven licensed beds, role throttles, one AudioContext and fallback/retry logic. Per-bed trims, cached sample peak normalization and a bounded master compressor address measured loudness disparity. Mute and visibility suspension remain authoritative.
5. Core art introduces transparent generated hero and immune assets. Higher-resolution backing compensates every scale and circle offset to preserve world geometry. Procedural art remains the missing-image fallback. Animation stays cosmetic and respects existing mutation/elite/boss signatures.
6. Menu and HUD gain restrained specimen framing, edges and bar highlights while keeping layout and touch geometry. No new blocking flow or settings are required.

## Acceptance

Focused contracts and typecheck per task, independent scoped review, final build and existing browser matrix: 100/150/200 enemies × WebGL/Canvas × full/reduced. Root inspects actual screenshots at portrait and compact sizes. Tests must assert body invariance, pool reuse/teardown, mute/stop races, hidden-context behavior and diagnostics opacity where affected. No readability threshold is weakened to obtain a pass.
